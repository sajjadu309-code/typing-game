🫧 Bubbles

How to play

Letters float up from the bottom in bubbles. Press the matching letter on your keyboard (or tap the bubble) before it reaches the top. A correct pop gives +10 points. A wrong key costs 5 points. Six escaped bubbles ends the game. Pop the goal number of bubbles to win. Every 5 pops the level goes up and the bubbles speed up.

🧲 KeyMagnet

How to play

The big box shows the target key. Press that key on your keyboard (or tap it on screen). A correct key gives +10 points. Wrong keys are counted and shown in red. The game ends when the timer reaches zero. Easy = 60 seconds, Medium = 45 seconds, Hard = 30 seconds. The key set decides which letters you practise.
